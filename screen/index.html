Screen account: this TV screen runs on a dedicated synagogue viewer account, screen-read-only, NEVER the super admin account. No finance, no admin, no editing from this screen. Super admin hanochhason@gmail.com is management only.

Burn-in prevention: content shifts gently on weekdays to protect a TV that runs 24/7. On Shabbat and holidays there is NO movement at all, including this shift.

Offline fallback, foundation: if this screen loses internet, it keeps showing the last info, with a last-updated stamp, and never goes blank. In this demo the last info is the sample data above. Change history: a previous announcement or message can be restored from the admin history.

Shabbat-safe mechanism, foundation: weekday-only content, payment text, and donation QR are hidden by the system on Shabbat and Yom Tov, not by relying on a gabbai remembering to flag them. See src/engines/shabbat-safe.js.
