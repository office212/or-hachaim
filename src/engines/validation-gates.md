# Times validation gates, foundation requirement

No unverified time is displayed live. No real data is imported
until these gates pass.

## Full-year validation, not one day

- Winter and summer, DST and standard time, seasonal changes.
- Holidays and fasts, including fast start and end detail.
- Dates with significant differences between calculation
  methods, including Shema Magen Avraham equal minutes versus
  seasonal, and candle lighting 18 minutes versus 20 minutes.
- Sephardic and Diaspora calendar: Yaaleh Veyavo, Barech Aleinu
  and Varechenu per chutz laaretz, Mashiv HaRuach and Morid
  HaGeshem or Morid HaTal, Sefirat HaOmer, Haftarot per Edot
  HaMizrach, holidays and special days, special Shabbatot,
  Rosh Chodesh, Shabbat Mevarchim, and Molad.

## Motzei Shabbat, angle verification

- Verify the angle itself, sun centre 8.5 degrees below the
  horizon, against MyZmanim on many dates, not only minute gaps.
- Published-minute range 41 to 52 minutes after sunset in
  Staten Island in 5787 is a check, not the definition.
- A mismatch on the angle is a gate failure, even if a minute
  gap happens to match on one date.

## Torah authority gate

- A Torah authority approves the full-year results and the
  Sephardic and Diaspora calendar behaviour before go-live.
- Until that approval, the screen shows sample or demo times
  only, clearly labeled, never as a published luach.

## Display and monitoring gates

- Offline fallback: last info continues, never a blank screen.
- Preview: gabbai sees the exact TV view, including the Shabbat
  view, before publishing.
- Validity: every announcement, sponsorship, and dedication has
  a start and end date, and shows only inside its window.
- Change history: a previous announcement or message can be
  restored.
- Display check: Hebrew and English text is not cut, and there
  are no RTL or LTR issues, at 1920x1080 landscape.
- Fault log and automatic monitoring: screen and finance errors
  are logged, with a periodic check that the screen is live and
  the backup ran. Monitoring is a foundation item, priority
  high, not a later add-on.
