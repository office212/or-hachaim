Or Hachaim / אור החייםFinance, TEST names onlyScreenGabbai admin

TEST DATA ONLY No real congregants. Ledger is append-only: corrections are correction entries, never deletes. Foundation: every financial action carries a unique operationId, and a duplicate submission is rejected. Pledge, charge, payment, and open balance are four distinct states, never conflated.

Add congregant / הוספת מתפלל

Email is optional. A congregant without email is fully managed here and cannot sign in until the super admin adds an email to this same existing record; history and balance are preserved.

Nedarim, fast entry for Motzei Shabbat / נדרים

One click per neder: name, amount, aliyah. A neder creates a ledger charge (mock, local only here). In the Firebase phase it is stored as a ledger entry with source "neder", so the append-only rules apply to it.

Recurring charges / חיובים קבועים

Monthly or annual membership fees are recorded automatically on schedule, with no manual entry each month. Pausing or cancelling a schedule stops future charges only; it never deletes past ledger entries.

Annual tax receipts, January / קבלות מס שנתיות

One click generates a print-ready 501c3 donation letter for every congregant for the year. This letter is separate from a balance statement. No PDF library is included yet, so this is a print-ready letter per congregant (window.print), clearly labeled as such, not a claimed PDF file. Open question: which payments count as tax-deductible donations is not decided here; the letter lists payments recorded in the year and says the gabbai must confirm the deductible portion before issuing a real letter, including any benefit received in return, such as meals or tickets, and the nonprofit eligibility to issue the letter.

Pledges, distinct from charges and payments / התחייבויות

Foundation: a pledge is a commitment, not a charge and not a payment. An unpaid pledge does not change the open balance until it is converted to a charge, and it is reported separately. A neder fast entry first creates a pledge here; converting it creates a ledger charge.

Annual financial report / דוח שנתי

Foundation, priority high: one annual report with total income, total charges, open balances, payments by payment method, and unpaid commitments. This report is separate from a balance statement and separate from a tax letter.

Activity log / יומן פעולות

Who changed what and when, for permissions, congregants, and ledger changes, not only the ledger itself. In the Firebase phase this log is append only in the database rules, like the ledger.

Backup from day one / גיבוי מהיום הראשון

Backup is a day-one requirement, not a go-live item. Export downloads the full ledger, congregant records, recurring schedules, and activity log (mock, local data only here). Restore never deletes or overwrites existing entries; it imports only missing entries. See src/firebase/backup-design.md and src/firebase/firestore.rules.
