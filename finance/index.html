היומן הוא append-only: תיקונים נעשים ברשומות תיקון, לעולם לא במחיקה. / Ledger is append-only: corrections are correction entries, never deletes. כל פעולה כספית נושאת operationId ייחודי, ושליחה כפולה נדחית. / Every financial action carries a unique operationId, and a duplicate submission is rejected. התחייבות, חיוב, תשלום, ויתרה פתוחה הם ארבעה מצבים נפרדים, לעולם לא מעורבבים. / Pledge, charge, payment, and open balance are four distinct states, never conflated.

הוספת מתפלל / Add congregant

מייל הוא אופציונלי. / Email is optional. A congregant without email is fully managed here and cannot sign in until the super admin adds an email to this same existing record; history and balance are preserved.

נדרים / Nedarim, fast entry for Motzei Shabbat

לחיצה אחת לכל נדר: שם, סכום, עלייה. / One click per neder: name, amount, aliyah. A neder creates a ledger charge, stored as a ledger entry with source "neder", so the append-only rules apply to it.

חיובים קבועים / Recurring charges

Monthly or annual membership fees are recorded automatically on schedule, with no manual entry each month. Pausing or cancelling a schedule stops future charges only; it never deletes past ledger entries.

קבלות מס שנתיות / Annual tax receipts, January

לחיצה אחת יוצרת מכתב תרומה 501c3 מוכן להדפסה לכל מתפלל לשנה. / One click generates a print-ready 501c3 donation letter for every congregant for the year. This letter is separate from a balance statement. No PDF library is included yet, so this is a print-ready letter per congregant (window.print), not a claimed PDF file. Which payments count as tax-deductible donations is not decided here; the letter lists payments recorded in the year and says the gabbai must confirm the deductible portion before issuing a real letter, including any benefit received in return, such as meals or tickets, and the nonprofit eligibility to issue the letter.

התחייבויות / Pledges, distinct from charges and payments

Foundation: a pledge is a commitment, not a charge and not a payment. An unpaid pledge does not change the open balance until it is converted to a charge, and it is reported separately. A neder fast entry first creates a pledge here; converting it creates a ledger charge.

דוח שנתי / Annual financial report

Foundation, priority high: one annual report with total income, total charges, open balances, payments by payment method, and unpaid commitments. This report is separate from a balance statement and separate from a tax letter.

יומן פעולות / Activity log

Who changed what and when, for permissions, congregants, and ledger changes, not only the ledger itself. In the Firebase phase this log is append only in the database rules, like the ledger.

גיבוי מהיום הראשון / Backup from day one

גיבוי הוא דרישה מהיום הראשון. / Backup is a day-one requirement, not a go-live item. Export downloads the full ledger, congregant records, recurring schedules, and activity log. Restore never deletes or overwrites existing entries; it imports only missing entries.
